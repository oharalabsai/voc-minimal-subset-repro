# Search Contract — voc-minimal-subset (PUBLIC; frozen at setup)

**Campaign kind:** search (counterexample/existence — not optimization)

**Objective:** Exhibit at least ONE measurement protocol using <=2 of the 3 modalities (SRPL, TRPL, transmission), at a strictly-smaller (Pareto-dominating) acquisition budget than the full three-measurement protocol, whose OWN inversion reproduces the full protocol's implied V_OC to within 10 mV worst-case, leave-one-device-out across the pinned synthetic p-i-n grid x certification noise seeds. Scope = INFORMATION SUFFICIENCY against the pinned forward model's own implied-V_OC output, NOT adequacy vs measured JV V_OC (the real-device ~170 mV stack offset from arXiv:2508.21037 is explicitly OUT of scope). A hit is verify()->valid:True. If exhaustive enumeration of all <=2-modality protocols under a strictly-smaller budget certifies ZERO passers, ship the certified negative (all three measurements are load-bearing for V_OC to 10 mV) plus the near-miss frontier (best reduced protocol's worst-case mV vs its budget).

**Search space:** A candidate is a PROTOCOL dict: {'modalities': <list, subset of ['srpl','trpl','transmission'], size 1-2>, 'configs': {<modality>: [<cfg>, ...]}}. Per-modality config menu (pinned in verifier.py): SRPL cfg = {'fluence': in (0.1,0.25,1.0) suns, 'window_ms': in (10,50,100)}; TRPL cfg = {'fluence': in (0.1,0.25,1.0), 'window_ns': in (50,100,500)}; TRANSMISSION cfg = {'window_ms': in (5,20)} (broadband probe, fixed flux 0.05 suns-equiv, no laser fluence). A modality may carry multiple configs (repeats/averaging lower that modality's measurement noise as sqrt(dose)); the budget accounting penalizes it. Budget = (total_acquisition_time_ms, total_integrated_photon_dose) computed by the pinned accounting: SRPL/TRANS time=sum(window_ms), TRPL time=sum(100/fluence); SRPL dose=sum(fluence*window_ms), TRANS dose=sum(0.05*window_ms), TRPL dose=sum(fluence*100). The FULL protocol (pinned reference) = SRPL(1.0,100ms)+TRPL(1.0,100ns)+TRANS(20ms) => (time=220, dose=201). The space is small and EXHAUSTIVELY enumerable: 6 non-empty subsets of size<=2, each with a bounded product of menu configs under the budget cap. The searcher enumerates candidates and calls verify(candidate) in-process; it edits ONLY the candidate dict, never the verifier or forward model.

**Constraints:** verify(candidate) returns valid:True iff ALL gates pass. G2: 1-2 modalities, all configs on the pinned menu. G3 (strictly-smaller budget): the candidate's (time,dose) must Pareto-dominate the full protocol's (220,201) -- time<=220 AND dose<=201 with at least one strict. G1 (recovery): worst-case over 24 pinned devices x 20 certification seeds (CERT_SEEDS=1000..1019, a namespace disjoint by construction from any screening seeds 0..999) of |implied_V_OC_reduced - implied_V_OC_full| <= 10 mV, leave-one-device-out. The reduced inversion sees ONLY the retained modalities' simulated (noisy) native quantities + protocol-INDEPENDENT pinned LODO population priors for any quantity whose measuring modality was dropped (Eg,d<-transmission; PLQE<-SRPL direct or TRPL indirect; EU<-SRPL/transmission) -- it NEVER sees any full-protocol intermediate or the dropped measurement's derived quantity. Ground truth for G1 is the FULL three-measurement protocol's OWN implied V_OC on the SAME device+seed (so shared retained modalities carry identical noise draws; the difference is exactly the information the dropped modality contributed). Pinned forward model (inside verifier.py): implied V_OC = V_OC,rad(Eg,Urbach E_U,thickness d) - kT*|ln PLQE|, with V_OC,rad from a detailed-balance ratio of an Urbach-broadened absorptance edge integrated against a Boltzmann emission tail (J0,rad) and a fixed solar generation spectrum (Jsc). Native measurement map (shot-noise-limited, sigma scales as sqrt(nominal_dose/actual_dose)): transmission->Eg (+/-2 meV nominal),d,E_U; SRPL->PLQE DIRECT (log-sigma 0.03 nominal) + E_U; TRPL->non-radiative rate reconstructing PLQE INDIRECTLY through the kinetic model (log-sigma 0.20 nominal -- an unbiased but far-less-precise second route, since it requires assumed injection level/radiative coefficient). Full-protocol PLQE = precision-weighted fusion of the SRPL-direct and TRPL-reconstructed estimates. G4 (determinism): verify is deterministic (seeded RandomState keyed by (seed,device,quantity); no hashing/threading nondeterminism) so the engine's double-run is byte-identical. Two setup-time integrity guards, both in verifier.py: dress_rehearsal() -- at infinite dose the full pipeline must reproduce the true implied V_OC to <1e-6 V, else the referee declares itself unfaithful and verify() RAISES (INCONCLUSIVE, never scores); pre_search_checks() -- reports full-protocol seed scatter (ILL-POSED-BAR kill if >10 mV) and per-modality LEVERAGE (mV that dropping each modality moves the full-protocol answer; a modality with leverage<=10 mV is reported non-load-bearing-by-construction, never sold as a discovered shortcut). Infra exceptions RAISE; only genuine candidate verdicts return a dict.

**Tools:** python, numpy

**Kill criteria:** DRESS-REHEARSAL / REFEREE kill: dress_rehearsal() cannot reproduce the full-protocol implied V_OC at infinite dose within 1e-6 V => verify() raises INCONCLUSIVE; the referee is unfaithful, fix the pipeline before grading any reduced protocol or kill.; ILL-POSED-BAR pre-search kill: pre_search_checks() full-protocol leave-one-device-out implied-V_OC seed scatter already exceeds 10 mV => the tolerance is unachievable in principle; kill and renegotiate the bar with the Director.; VACUOUS-GRID handling: pre_search_checks() leverage shows a modality moves full-protocol implied V_OC by <=10 mV across the grid => that reduction is trivially free; report the modality as non-load-bearing-by-construction (TRPL is expected here -- redundant with SRPL for the V_OC scalar), NEVER as a discovered shortcut.
